Other settings

Claude only sees tab titles and URLs with query strings and fragments removed.

All windows: tabs from every window are grouped together and moved into the window you run it from. Tabs that don't end up in a group stay where they are. A window left with no tabs closes.

When a new tab has no matching group in its own window but does in another, move it there and switch to that window. Off: new tabs always stay in the window they opened in.
